# 0044 What is finished can be left out of what is open

Builds on [0043](0043-a-large-tree-folds-around-your-work.md). Status: built.

An open item drew everything under it, finished or not. 0043 left that as it was: finished items beside each other keep a line each, and a feature with forty finished stories is forty lines. Far into a piece of work, most of what is under an open story is ticks, and the two tasks that remain are read among them. Folding does not help: a fold is all of an item or none of it.

**Decision**

- **`d` leaves what is finished out of whatever is open, and `d` again draws it.** A finished task goes from its story; a story with everything landed goes from its feature, with what is under it. It is one choice for the whole tree, as the order is, and this person's, as a fold is: it holds whatever the room.
- **Finished is done with nothing left in it for anyone.** Done, or with everything under it landed, and its helpers done. What something in still asks a person, what an agent still works in, and what the session open here is in, stay drawn, and so does the way to them.
- **What is left out is counted where it was.** `✓3` after a name: three finished things under it are not drawn. The top of the screen counts all of them, which also says that the tree is not all there is. The panel still lists everything under what is selected.
- **The tree is as much narrower, and is folded less to fit.** What is left out is left out before anything is folded for the room, so a tree may fit whole that did not.
- **What is selected is still something drawn.** When what was selected goes, what it was under is selected. `/` finds what is left out too: the foot says that `enter` will show what is finished, and `enter` draws it again and arrives.
- **It moves as a fold does.** Pressing `d`, and a task landing while finished work is left out, move the tree from one shape to the other.
- **`canopy.finished` says which the canopy opens with.** `shown` is the default.

**What it comes to**

The tree of the tests, 31 tips of which 11 have landed, is 209 columns whole and 189 with what is finished left out: in a window of 195 it no longer folds anything to fit. On a generated project of 2,217 items a frame is 4 ms either way.

**Not done**

- It is all of the tree or none of it. There is no leaving out under one item only.
- Going to a finished thing by name draws everything finished again, not that thing alone.
- What an agent finished since this person looked goes with the rest. `u` still goes to it: to what it was under, with who and why at the foot.
- Drawing it again does not put the selection back where it was.
- A folded line says how far along it is as before: `24/80` counts what is finished in it.

**Evidence**

`packages/canopy/test/finished.test.ts`: what goes and what is counted, and the same screen again after the second `d`; what stays for asking, for being worked in, for a helper not done, for being where this person is; where the selection goes; a folded line; going to a name that is left out and to one that is not; a room in which the tree fits whole only with finished work left out; the foot and the setting.
