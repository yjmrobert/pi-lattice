pi-lattice · packages/pane · study

The view from above

Today the pane shows sessions: what each one is doing and what it needs from you. These five screens step back to the work itself, as a staff lead sees it next to other staff leads: who holds what, what waits on whom, and what lands when. All five draw the same invented network (four leads, five repositories, eight workstreams), so they can be compared directly. Click anything in a screen to inspect it; keys 1 to 5 switch between them.

1 · Where is the work flowing?

Dendrite

The network is a cell body. Each workstream is a branch that reaches as far as its phase, from brief to landed, and its sessions are the twigs. Pulses run along the branches that are moving. A health panel counts what finishes each day.

dendrite.html →
2 · Who is holding what?

Orbits

One system per lead. A workstream orbits its owner, closer in as it nears landing, with its sessions as moons. The lines that cross between systems are the point: a wait, an offer, a question nobody is around to answer.

orbit.html →
3 · When does it land?

Timeline

Time runs left to right with today as a fixed line. One lane per lead, one bar per workstream. What is behind the line happened; what is ahead is a forecast. A dashed drop shows a session that starts when another ends.

timeline.html →
4 · Where is it happening?

Lattice

Leads down, repositories across, and the work where they cross, one glyph per session. It is the most literal of the five and it is plain text, so Pi could draw it exactly as shown.

lattice.html →
5 · What do we owe each other?

Ledger

No map. Three columns of obligations between leads: what you owe, what you are owed, and where you could help. Each item is answered in place, as in the pane today. Underneath runs a wire of what just moved.

All five rest on one idea the pane does not have yet: a workstream, meaning a root session and everything under it, treated as one unit with an owner, a phase and a risk.
